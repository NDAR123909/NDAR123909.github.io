Other projects

ou-statarb

Statistical arbitrage research framework. Python, sole author.

A pairs-trading research library that models the spread between two cointegrated stocks as an Ornstein–Uhlenbeck process, with a Kalman-filtered hedge ratio and a backtester that charges trading costs. A naive scan of 465 pairs flags 74 as cointegrated where about 23 are expected by chance, so pair selection uses Benjamini–Hochberg FDR control. Over 19 walk-forward folds on 31 DJIA stocks (2006–2017), I report the median fold's out-of-sample Sharpe, 0.18, not the best backtest.

ENSO climate data pipeline

1st place, 2026 UA Data Visualization Challenge

An ETL pipeline that brings five federal climate and hydrology sources (NOAA, NCEP/NCAR, USGS, NASA) into a seven-table PostgreSQL star schema. It feeds a D3.js visualization of 75 years of El Niño and La Niña events against river discharge in the western U.S.

LTP Liquidity Arena 2026

Track A finalist. Live-capital algorithmic trading competition, Jul 2026 – present.

An autonomous agent trading live capital on Binance USDT-margined perpetuals, running OU mean reversion on FDR-screened cointegrated pairs from ou-statarb. Risk controls include volatility-targeted sizing, a kill switch at 12% off peak, and a 2× NAV gross cap. In Phase 1 it placed 6th of 30 advancing teams with the board's highest Sharpe, 4.86: +3.8% return and 3.7% max drawdown over 32 days.
